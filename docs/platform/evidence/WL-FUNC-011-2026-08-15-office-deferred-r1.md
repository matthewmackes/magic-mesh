# WL-FUNC-011 native office editing disposition r1

Date: 2026-08-15

The operator selected “defer office editing and close this requirement.” The
native Editor remains the supported text/code surface. Office containers remain
behind the existing bounded, symlink-safe admission boundary and are refused
before UTF-8 loading when the approved LibreOfficeKit runtime is unavailable.

This disposition does not claim office open/edit/save/recovery proof and does
not authorize a VCL, GTK, `soffice`, or host-process fallback. The requirement
may be reopened when a sandboxed, package-owned LibreOfficeKit adapter and its
runtime artifacts are available.
